项目简介

Rapid bay is a self hosted video service/torrent client that makes playing videos from torrents as easy as: 1. Opening the webapp on a phone/laptop/tablet/smartTV. 2. Searching for content. 3. Selecting desired video file. 4. Waiting for Download/Conversion. 5. Playing on the device or cast to AppleTV/Chromecast - Uses [Jackett](https://github.com/Jackett/Jackett) and/or [Prowlarr](https://github.com/Prowlarr/Prowlarr) as a search backend. - Pick individual video files you want to play...

Videostreaming service/torrent client that allows searching and playing videos from torrents in the browser or from a Chromecast/AppleTV/Smart TV. `MIT` `Python/Docker`
